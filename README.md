# Retail Customer Data Analysis | Excel, Python, SQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail customer and transaction data to uncover meaningful business insights, understand customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate overall sales performance.

I followed an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Initial data exploration and EDA.
- **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning, preprocessing, and EDA.
- **MySQL:** Data querying and business analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and visualization.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and analytical measures.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Initial Data Exploration – Excel
- Explored the dataset structure.
- Examined columns and data patterns.
- Identified potential data quality issues.

### 2. Data Cleaning & EDA – Python
- Imported data using Pandas.
- Handled missing values and duplicate records.
- Cleaned and standardized data.
- Performed exploratory data analysis.

### 3. Business Analysis – SQL
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into MySQL.
- Wrote SQL queries to analyze sales and customer behavior.
- Analyzed country-wise revenue, customers, and orders.
- Extracted meaningful business insights.

### 4. Dashboard Development – Power BI
- Connected the analyzed data to Power BI.
- Created DAX measures for KPIs.
- Developed interactive charts and visualizations.
- Analyzed country-wise revenue contribution.
- Evaluated monthly revenue trends.

## Key Business Insights

1. **United Kingdom – Leading Revenue Contributor:** Among 40+ countries, the UK generated approximately **89.5% of total revenue**.

2. **Highest Customer Base:** The UK recorded the highest number of customers.

3. **Highest Order Volume:** The UK also generated the highest number of orders.

4. **Declining Monthly Revenue:** Revenue showed a declining trend across the months, indicating a potential slowdown in business performance.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Market Diversification:** Explore opportunities in other countries to reduce dependency on the UK market.

2. **Investigate Revenue Decline:** Analyze monthly orders, average order value, and customer purchasing patterns to identify possible reasons.

3. **Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted marketing campaigns.

4. **International Growth:** Develop country-specific marketing strategies to improve sales in underperforming markets.

5. **Performance Monitoring:** Regularly monitor revenue, order volume, and customer activity through Power BI dashboards.
